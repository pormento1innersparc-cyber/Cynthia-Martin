Know Your Options

Bank Financing vs Pag-IBIG Financing

Bank Financing

  • Higher loan amount
  • Longer payment terms
  • Flexible payment options
  • Suitable for investment and rental properties

Pag-IBIG Financing

  • Lower interest rates
  • Fixed monthly amortization
  • Designed for end-user homebuyers
  • Easier and more accessible application process
