Know Your Options

Bank Financing vs Pag-IBIG Financing

Bank Financing

  • May sariling requirements at qualification process ang bawat bangko
  • Iba-iba ang loanable amount depende sa income documents mo
  • May mga flexible na payment options depende sa bangko
  • Karaniwang pinipili para sa investment o rental properties

Pag-IBIG Financing

  • May sariling membership at contribution requirements
  • Fixed ang monthly amortization
  • Karaniwang para ito sa end-user na titira mismo sa bahay
  • May sariling application process at loan structure
"Magkaiba ang requirements, qualification, terms, rates, at loan structures — alamin kung alin ang mas bagay sa budget at income mo."
